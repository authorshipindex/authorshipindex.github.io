Contact

This standard holds no records, so there is nothing to look up, correct or delete. Most questions are answered by the standard itself.

Getting in touch

For questions about the standard, corrections to these pages, or a proposed ruling: —

Publicly, the standard is discussed at @authorshipindex. That is where it is talked about; this is where it is administered.

Replies come from a person and may take some time.

Matters requiring no enquiry

Permission to use the mark. You have it, permanently and without asking. See the licence.

To register a work. There is no register. Print the mark and it works.

To correct a declaration. Nothing is stored here to correct. A mark is fixed at the moment of printing; declare the next edition differently.

To remove your data. There is none.

Disputes about a specific work

This standard hosts no opinion about any declaration and operates no dispute process. Readers who doubt a mark do what readers have always done: discuss it, wherever that kind of work is already discussed. Writing here will not change a number on somebody else’s cover.
